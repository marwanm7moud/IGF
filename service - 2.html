Debt Collection Service

Efficient and Professional Debt Collection

We offer comprehensive debt collection services to help you recover outstanding payments efficiently and legally.

What We Offer

Our debt collection service is designed to assist businesses and individuals in recovering owed amounts with professionalism and care. We take a structured approach to resolve disputes and ensure that debts are recovered in a timely manner.

  • Debt collection for individuals and companies
  • Amicable negotiation to resolve outstanding debts
  • Legal action for defaulting clients
  • International debt collection services
  • Experienced and trained collection agents

Debt Collection Fees

Our fees for debt collection depend on the amount collected and the nature of the case. We offer transparent pricing to ensure that your financial interests are well-protected throughout the collection process.

  • 7% to 20% of the total debt collected (varies by case complexity)
  • Legal fees for debt recovery proceedings: 2% of the debt amount, with a maximum of 1000 EGP (or a minimum of 500 EGP)
  • Sundry expenses: 100 EGP per case referred to us
  • International debt collection service: Fees to be discussed based on case specifics

Debt Collection Process

Our process ensures that debts are collected fairly and effectively. We employ a professional approach to track down debtors and work towards a favorable resolution.

  1. Initial contact and negotiation with the debtor
  2. Sending a formal notice for outstanding payments
  3. Payment negotiation and settlement arrangement
  4. Legal action for unresolved debts, including filing lawsuits
  5. Enforcement of court decisions and debt recovery through legal channels
