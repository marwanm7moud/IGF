Our Fees

Transparent Pricing for Our Financial Services

We offer competitive and clear pricing to ensure transparency. Below are the fees for our various services.

Debt Collection Fees

Our debt collection services are priced based on the amount collected. We aim to offer cost-effective solutions to ensure your collections are handled efficiently.

  • 7% to 20% of the amount collected
  • Filing fees for legal action: 2% of debt value, with a maximum of 1000 EGP (or 500 EGP minimum)
  • Sundry expenses: 100 EGP per case referred to us

Verification Services Fees

We offer detailed verification services for both retail and corporate clients. Below are the fees based on location and service type.

  • 35 EGP for verification visits in Cairo and Alexandria
  • 45 EGP for verification visits in other governorates (except for Hurghada, Upper Egypt, etc.)
  • 70 EGP for special provinces like Hurghada, Assiut, Luxor, etc.
  • 300 EGP for corporate verification in Cairo
  • 350 EGP for corporate verification in Alexandria, Delta, Fayoum, Beni Suef
  • 400 EGP for corporate verification in Upper Egypt and Hurghada

Traffic Points Inspection Fees

Our inspection services for auto loans include vehicle checks at traffic points to ensure compliance with loan agreements.

  • 70 EGP per car inspection at traffic points

Data Certificate Issuance Fees

We provide certificates for vehicles under bank loans to verify their details and ownership status.

  • 55 EGP per data certificate issued

Data Update Fees

We offer data update services for clients to ensure their contact details are accurate and up-to-date.

  • 60 EGP for each client data update

Client Disclosure Fees

Our client disclosure service helps banks access essential information about their clients to ensure sound decision-making.

  • 50 EGP per client disclosure
